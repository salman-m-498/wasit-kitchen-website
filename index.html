About Us

Who We Are

Wasit Kitchen Supply provides world-class kitchen equipment and solutions to restaurants, hotels and healthcare in the Gulf region, with strict adherence to efficiency, safety and environmental considerations.

Wasit Kitchen Equipment has over two decades of experience in providing design, supply and installation solutions for industrial and commercial kitchens across the UAE. With expertise in custom-fabricated equipment and partnerships with leading manufacturers from Europe, the USA and Italy, the company delivers high-quality kitchen solutions tailored to client needs.

Wasit also supplies, imports and exports high-quality kitchen equipment, carefully selected to keep pace with modern styles and customer needs — committed to reliable service and strong after-sales support.
